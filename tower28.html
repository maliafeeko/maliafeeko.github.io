What I Do

I help shape what you see on Tower 28’s TikTok and Instagram. I plan our monthly content calendars and make short-form content for every corner of the feed, from everyday evergreen posts to TikTok Shop pushes and product launches. I also run our IG Broadcast Channel and our 70K-member TYB community, where I lead the challenges and activations that get people involved, and I help with brand and influencer partnerships. Most days I’m filming and editing trend-driven videos, planning community events, and checking what’s working so the next post does even better.
